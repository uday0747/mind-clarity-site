# Website mission revamp — 10 October 2026

User asked for a site-wide presentation centered on making God understandable through the Creator/game story, applying remembrance in life, respecting other religions, and challenging manipulation in God’s name. Publication is authorized.

The homepage now leads with the story and purpose before problem guides. A shared reading journey links every educational page to explanation, practice, and recognition of others. Philosophy explains the social purpose; practice adds a faith/division example; FAQ adds four questions. Repeated proof-oriented introductory wording is replaced by explanation and application. Scripture remains attributed to its specific teaching, with the distinction between Ashtavakra’s verses and Uday’s game story retained.

“Visible” means imaginable to the mind and recognized through living beings, rather than a claim of a physically demonstrated divine being. Peace is the guiding hope. No claim that this is humanity’s only hope or that the website guarantees peace is added. No religion, country, or orphanage is accused collectively. Original Mula Siddhanta and governing statement are unchanged. Medical, safety, consent, and boundary guidance remains.

All new source copy is listed in site-revamp-strings.json and translated fully across seven language dictionaries. Core landing metadata is updated. Build, route, metadata, translation completeness and browser checks precede publication.
