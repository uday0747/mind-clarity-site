## Website

Homepage and Questions page show all ten categories and all 59 terms, with links to the full guide. The guide provides an explanation and a relevant destination for every group. Health categories contain a visible qualification even on the homepage; wider social concerns explicitly require resources, cooperation and safeguards. The site presents reflection alongside healthcare, not prevention or treatment of depression, illness, fatigue or sleep difficulties.

Three new pages: life-problems-and-self-enquiry, confidence-self-worth-and-self-enquiry, overthinking-worry-and-uncertainty. All three have full versions in Telugu, Hindi, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Indonesian and Arabic. New translations were editorially prepared, not independently native-speaker certified.
