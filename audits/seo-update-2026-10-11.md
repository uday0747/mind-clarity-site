## Reference and scope

Google Search Central guidance used:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link

Sitemap dates describe verified content, link and metadata updates; they are not reset merely by running a build. Character lengths are an editorial check, not a guaranteed search-snippet display limit. Search Console submission/indexing status, rankings and traffic were not accessed. Publication does not establish that search engines have already recrawled or changed their displayed results.
