# Teaching and reading journey revamp

The four original statements of 15 October are preserved unchanged in the raw archive. This website revision is an editorial adaptation, not a transcript or a claim that astrology proves the philosophy.

The homepage now proceeds through shared identity, voluntary limitation, participation in the design, time and astrology, examination of the reasoning, and everyday connection. Existing benefits, Yuga vision, introduction videos and deeper reading remain. Astrology and the distinction between observation and interpretation are also integrated into Understanding God, Philosophy, Questions and the enquiry guides. Practice adds connection during conflict and enjoyment of ordinary nature.
