All four live learning pages returned HTTP 200. Their main content matched the local production HTML exactly. The gaps below therefore exist in the published website, not only in an old local copy.

## Governing statement and teaching sequence

The governing statement to communicate as Uday's spiritual philosophy is:

> We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.

“We” refers to the one shared Creator appearing through different lives and roles. This is distinct from claiming that an individual personality can control events or other people.

The intended sequence for each teaching is:

1. State the relevant part of that central understanding.
2. Identify the everyday scene and the emotion or identification it brings.
3. Explain how remembering the Creator and the already-designed game changes the interpretation of the scene.
4. Relate participation, relationships, and the next action to that understanding; learning and acting are themselves within the unfolding game.
5. Ask the reader to revisit the central understanding and examine what they actually noticed.

An exercise should make its connection explicit through explanation. Merely appending “remember you are the Creator” to otherwise unrelated advice does not establish that connection. Practical observations and analogies can illustrate the philosophy; they cannot independently prove that people created the universe or that its entire course is predetermined.

## Missing earlier requests and project continuity

- The movie/theatre analogy is missing from both the current reference document and all four live learning pages. It was previously reported as included.
- The money, gold, diamonds, and relationship example requested by Uday is missing. It would need to connect possessions as small parts of creation with recognition of the same Creator in people, rather than making financial or relationship guarantees.
- Sthitaprajna appears in the reference document but has no dedicated explanation on the website.
- README still points to the old version 1.1 website direction and a general idea-example-action model. The previously reported update to project guidance was not applied.
- The homepage and Daily Practice page omit the fixed-design statement entirely. It appears in the Philosophy root statement and the FAQ's effort question.
- Contact, privacy, and payment details are supporting information. They should remain accurate factual information, rather than being turned into philosophical arguments.

## Example of the revised connection

For the unanswered-message example, a coherent explanation within the philosophy would be:

> A reply has not arrived, and the role you are playing feels rejected. Remember the teaching: we are the Creator who entered this already-designed game for experience. Waiting, the feeling, and the wish to control the next scene all arise within it. You do not know the next scene, and cannot stand outside the game to rewrite its design. The other person is another expression of the same Creator. Notice whether remembering this loosens your identification with rejection. You can still send a clear follow-up; that action, too, belongs to the unfolding game.

The reflection can then ask what the person felt, what they were demanding from the scene, what the central remembrance means to them, and what changed or did not change. This keeps the philosophical connection explicit without treating a feeling of calm as proof of the cosmology.

## Completion standard for a future revision

Every teaching section should establish its connection to the complete governing statement. Every worked example should demonstrate that connection within its own explanation. Every reflection should return to it and invite examination rather than require a predetermined answer. Summaries, metadata, the reference document, and future-content guidance should agree on the same formulation. Technical build checks alone cannot establish this editorial coherence.

## Revision outcome — 23 September 2026

The authorized revision replaces the disconnected teaching sequences across the four learning pages and the organized reference document. The homepage now leads with the complete governing statement. Thirteen philosophy lessons connect their explanations and reflections to the Creator within the game; the practice page uses five shared steps and six full examples. The movie/lotus, possessions/relationships, steady-wisdom, four-path, and Beautiful World explanations are connected to the same root. Care is explained as arising from recognition, rather than as an independent moral command. Contact, payment, and privacy remain factual support information.

Reference version 1.5 and README now record the complete teaching and its acceptance criteria for future website and YouTube work. The central statement, original Mula Siddhanta, five steps, and six examples are shared through `teaching-content.json`. The twelve visible questions and their structured data share `questions.js`. The retained historical Beautiful World guide was not changed.
