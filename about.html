About Uday

A lifelong question: Who am I?

I am Pinninti Uday Kumar, a Mind Clarity Consultant based in Telangana, India. My enquiry into identity and the nature of experience has shaped the way I approach life and relationships.

Through Mind Clarity, I share my perspective on self-understanding, witnessing and compassion. My purpose is to help make the world a more beautiful place to live while encouraging thoughtful participation in everyday responsibilities.

My approach invites discussion. You are welcome to ask questions and hold a different perspective.
