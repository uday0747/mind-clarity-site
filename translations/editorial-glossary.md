# Mind Clarity translation glossary and review guide

11 October 2026. This is an editorial guide, not certification by native speakers.

| Term | Meaning to preserve | Avoid implying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator / God / Shakti | Uday’s proposed shared source appearing through every life | Each separate personality has supernatural powers |
| Game of experiences | Uday’s account of a creation designed before entry and experienced through roles | Suffering is unreal or unimportant |
| Role | A changing personal identity, relationship or situation within experience | A person’s rights or needs can be ignored |
| Fixed design | The whole cannot be rewritten from inside; decisions and effort unfold within it | There is no reason to act, learn or repair harm |
| Remembrance | Returning to the whole Creator-and-game understanding | A compulsory affirmation, schedule or special feeling |
| Self-enquiry | Honest questioning of identity and the teaching | Required journaling, passive obedience or a guaranteed result |
| Surrender | Participation without demanding control of the whole | Accepting abuse or surrendering consent and boundaries |
| Realization / enlightenment | The understanding described in this teaching | Guaranteed health, wealth, perfect judgement or superiority |
| Natural care | Recognition of the same source in others, alongside practical responsibility | A substitute for accountability, lawful help or professional care |
| Written discussions | Preserved text used as the source for editorial adaptation | Verified audio or video recordings |

## Native editor checklist

For Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Indonesian, Arabic, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu, review the homepage premise, Philosophy’s root statement, surrender/effort, responsibility and boundaries, health passages, optional payment information, and the new privacy choices. Compare with the English source and preserved original statements. Record reviewer, language, pages, date and corrections before describing a translation as independently reviewed.

Check that God/Creator/Shakti remain consistent within their context; distinguish quoted original statements, editorial explanations and external scripture translations. Preserve uncertainty and qualifications. Check natural question-finder queries, status messages, mobile line breaks and Arabic directionality. Source proper names and source-edition language may remain untranslated.

No native-editor certification has been supplied for this release. Search Console and GA4 account reports also require an authenticated account review; source and route checks do not establish traffic, indexing or field performance.

12 October 2026: German, Italian, Russian, Japanese and Korean were added after targeted sample correction. Samples exposed machine mistranslations of forgiveness and surrender, so stronger translation support must not be treated as certification. Known Tamil journal wording and corrupt Odia word fragments were repaired. The full Odia root quotation was restored. Independent full semantic review remains pending.
