## Second post — waiting for a reply

“We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.”

Here is one way I explore this understanding.

You send a message. No reply comes. Soon the mind says, “They do not care about me.” The role of friend or partner begins to feel like your whole identity.

In this philosophy, both people express the same Creator. Waiting, uncertainty, and whatever response follows belong to the game. We cannot dictate its next scene, and we do not yet know why the reply has not arrived.

A considerate follow-up or returning to the work in front of you can also be part of that experience.

Reflection: Which role feels hurt? What response am I demanding? What happens when I remember our shared source?

You may notice a change, or none. Observe honestly.

Explore the practice: https://mindclarity.co.in/practice

## Third post — does a fixed design remove responsibility?

“We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.”

A natural question is: then why make any effort?

In my understanding, deciding, helping, learning, and repairing mistakes all occur within that design.

Suppose you forget a commitment and someone is inconvenienced. You cannot replace what happened. You can experience remorse, acknowledge the effect, and make a realistic new arrangement. That response is also part of the unfolding game.

Recognizing the same Creator in the other person gives care for that relationship its meaning. The mistake belongs to your experience; it does not contain your whole identity.

Reflection: Can I remember this shared identity while honestly meeting the consequences of what happened?

More questions and explanations: https://mindclarity.co.in/faq
