## Recording the first Short

Start with `01-a-mistake-is-not-your-whole-identity.txt`. Read only its text, using your ordinary speaking voice. The other two scripts are prepared so the series has a consistent direction, but record the first one before recording all three.

Uday has confirmed English for all three recordings. The narration uses the same terms as the website.

1. Use your phone's usual voice recorder in a quiet room, with curtains or other soft furnishings if available.
2. Place the phone roughly a handspan from your mouth, slightly to one side. Make a short test and listen for clarity before the full take.
3. Leave about two seconds of quiet before and after speaking. Read calmly and allow a small pause between paragraphs. No music is needed in the recording.
4. If you stumble, pause and repeat that sentence. Keep going; there is no need to restart the entire take.
5. Attach the original audio file to this task. M4A, WAV, or MP3 is suitable. Recording only audio is enough; no camera appearance is needed.

The scripts aim for approximately 60–75 seconds at a natural, unhurried pace. This is a planning estimate, not a measured duration. The actual recording will determine the edit. If the first take is longer, assess wording and pacing with Uday; do not speed up his voice or distort the teaching to meet an arbitrary length.
