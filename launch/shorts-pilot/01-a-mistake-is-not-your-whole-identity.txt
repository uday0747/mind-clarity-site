A missed promise can become a harsh identity: “I am a failure.”

Here is the understanding I explore in Mind Clarity.

We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.

By Creator, I mean the shared source in every life.

Forgetting a call is an experience in a role. It is not the whole of who you are.

You cannot undo that scene. Yet apologising and making a new arrangement are also part of the game.

Recognising the same Creator in the person affected gives that care its meaning.

Can you acknowledge the mistake without making it your whole identity?

Pause, remember, and notice honestly.
