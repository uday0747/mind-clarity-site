You send a message. Hours pass. The mind says, “They don't care.”

In Mind Clarity, the understanding is this:

We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.

The Creator is the shared source in both lives.

The role of friend feels rejected. But you do not yet know why the reply has not come.

You cannot dictate the next scene. A considerate follow-up, or returning to what you were doing, is also participation in the game.

What reply are you demanding? Can you remember the same Creator in the other person while you wait?
