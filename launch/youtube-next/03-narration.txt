A lotus remains in the pond. In Uday Kumar's teaching, it is an image of living fully, inwardly untouched.

The understanding behind that image is this:

We are the Creator. We created this game of experiences and entered it. Having designed it before entering, we cannot change its design from inside the game.

The Creator is the shared source in every life.

When someone interrupts you, anger may appear. You can feel it and still say, “Let me finish, then I will listen.”

Remembering our shared source can inform that response. The feeling, the boundary, and the listening all belong to the game.

Can you stay involved without making this passing role your whole identity?

Explore that gently. There is no deadline for a feeling to leave. The free reflection is linked on this channel's profile.
